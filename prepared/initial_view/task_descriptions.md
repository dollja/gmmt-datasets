# Fixed task descriptions and verification status

## MapTask (`maptask`)

One participant describes a route, and another attempts to reproduce it on their own map. Their maps may differ, and neither participant can see the other's map.

This wording comes from the supplied task instructions. Corresponding map pairs are inventoried in the conversation index, but the images have not yet been acquired.

## Werewolf variants

The dataset authors identify One Night Ultimate Werewolf and The Resistance: Avalon. Their paper's Appendix A verifies the broad structures below. These are fixed draft descriptions, not certification of individual games' role decks or house rules. Do not apply generic multi-night elimination Werewolf rules.

`one_night_ultimate_werewolf`: Players have secret roles. Role-dependent night actions may change cards. Players then discuss identities and vote; the village seeks to eliminate a werewolf, while werewolves try to avoid elimination.

`resistance_avalon`: Players have secret allegiances. A rotating leader proposes a quest team, players approve or reject it, and the approved team resolves the quest. Good seeks three successful quests; evil seeks three failures or identifies Merlin after good's quest success.

Source: https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.411.pdf , Appendix A, printed pp. 6580–6581. Per-game variant remains unverified until the actual public setup is inspected. Exact voting/tie/no-werewolf cases, role abilities and night order require the matching rulebook and setup; do not infer them from the title or hidden-role table. Keep such games pending rather than supply an incorrect fixed description.
